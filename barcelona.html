Barcelona, Spain

Exploring Aotearoa About Me Home International Adventures

While in Dublin my partner and I chose to go to Barcelona for three nights. The heat, the beach and Razzmatazz were all highlights for me. Razzmatazz is a five-storey nightclub that opened at midnight, we were there till 5am. It was a great time, each storey had a different genre of music, and it felt like an indoors music festival. This is probably one of my favourite places I’ve visited, mainly because of how different it is to anywhere I’ve ever been before. The streets were so narrow and the buildings so tall, it really helped you keep cool while walking during the day.
